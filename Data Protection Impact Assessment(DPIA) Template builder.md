**DATA PROTECTION IMPACT ASSESSMENT (DPIA) TEMPLATE**

Reusable compliance template aligned to GDPR and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), with the DPDP Rules, 2025 considered where relevant.

Note:

Terminology: GDPR controller = DPDP Data Fiduciary; data subject = Data Principal; supervisory authority = Data Protection Board of India (the Board), special category data has no direct DPDP equivalent. This template uses "Organisation" and "Individuals" to cover both.

**Not a legal advice:** This template supports compliance documentation. Have qualified counsel or your DPO confirm how the law applies to your facts.

**DPDP Consent checklist (if relying on consent):**

- Free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous, with a clear affirmative action
- Limited to personal data necessary for the specific purpose
- Notice given before or at the time of the consent request, in clear and plain language (sec 5; Rule 3), with itemised data and purpose and how to withdraw and complain
- Withdrawal is as easy as giving consent
- Consent Manager option supported where applicable (Rule 4)
- Verifiable parental / guardian consent where children or persons with a lawful guardian are involved (sec 9; Rules 10-11)

1. **Risk Identification and Assessment**
   1. Scoring Method

**Likelihood (L) and Severity (S) are each scored 1-5. Inherent risk = L x S**

| **Score** | **Likelihood** | **Severity (impact on individuals)**     |
| --------- | -------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| 1         | Rare           | Negligible, minor inconvenience          |
| 2         | Unlikely       | Limited, recoverable                     |
| 3         | Possible       | Significant, recoverable with difficulty |
| 4         | Likely         | Serious, hard to reverse                 |
| 5         | Almost certain | Severe, irreversible damage              |

**Rating bands**  
Low : - 1 – 4

Medium : 5 – 9

High : 10 – 16

Very High : 17 – 25
